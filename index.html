Hobbies 📚

I love playing Volleyball, relaxing with K-dramas, getting creative with art and crafts, and experimenting in the kitchen.

Playing Volleyball

Volleyball has always been more than just a sport; it was the pulsing beat of my childhood, beginning around age nine when I first discovered the thrill of playing. The game taught me the invaluable lesson to cherish every moment in the larger game of life, and this is my TOTGA.

Watching Korean Dramas

My curiosity was sparked when I saw my sister or a close friend watching a colorful, emotional series around the time I was starting middle school. Watching K-dramas soon became a secret, cozy ritual, often involving sneaking snacks into my room and staying up late to catch the next episode. It taught me patience (while waiting for subtitles) and captivated me instantly.

Doing Arts & Craft

Arts is a big part of my life; it creates dedication that taught me to discover "What can I do more?" It started when I was around eight or nine years old; I started creating things by hand using some papers and other art materials, and when I entered high school, I explored more, and now, I use my talent to have my own small crafting business.

Cooking Different Dishes

My love affair with cooking began not in a formal kitchen, but at the side of my grandmother when I was around senior high school. The joy of experimenting with flavors and techniques, or perhaps even the comfort and stress relief found in the structured process of following a recipe. And now, a handful of simple ingredients could be transformed into something that brought immediate joy to the dinner table.
